## Search Intent & Funnel Mapping

### Top of Funnel (TOFU) — Informational Intent
- **Queries:** "Difference between energy and exergy", "What is pinch analysis in industrial engineering", "How to calculate chiller plant kW/TR".
- **Landing Pages:** `/approach`, `/applications`, `/about`.
- **Target Action:** Educate facility managers and technical directors on Second-Law exergy loss, guiding them toward the interactive facility assessment tool.

### Middle of Funnel (MOFU) — Commercial Investigation
- **Queries:** "Industrial energy saving consultancy Dubai", "Boiler heat recovery systems UAE", "Industrial drying optimization paper manufacturing", "District cooling efficiency GCC".
- **Landing Pages:** `/services`, `/services/[slug]`, `/industries`.
- **Target Action:** Deep-dive into specific thermodynamic specs, case study metrics (e.g. 42% steam cut), and engineering principles.

### Bottom of Funnel (BOFU) — High-Intent Transactional
- **Queries:** "Book industrial energy audit Dubai", "Guaranteed energy savings contract UAE", "Exergy Solutions contact".
- **Landing Pages:** `/assessment`, `/contact`.
- **Target Action:** Complete multi-step assessment audit or connect directly with the engineering desk.

## Content Gap & Internal Linking Architecture

1. **Hub-and-Spoke Topology:**
   - **Central Hubs:** `/services` and `/industries`.
   - **Spokes:** The 9 individual service pages (`/services/cooling`, `/services/heating`, etc.) link back to `/services`, to related industries (e.g. Pulp & Paper, Petrochemicals), and straight into the assessment form with prefilled query parameters (`/assessment?service=cooling`).
2. **Contextual In-Content Cross-Links:**
   - On `/about`: Direct links to `/approach` (Methodology) and `/applications` (Real-world results).
   - On `/approach`: Links to `/services/waste-heat` and `/services/process` demonstrating pinch analysis in practice.
   - On `/services/[slug]`: Cross-links to related industrial sectors with sector-specific challenges.
3. **Anchor Text Strategy:**
   - Avoid generic "click here" or "learn more".
   - Use descriptive, semantic anchors: `cooling optimization`, `Second-Law exergy analysis`, `42% steam reduction case study`, `facility assessment tool`.
