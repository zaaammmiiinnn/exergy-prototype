# Phase 2 Parking Lot & Future Candidates

Items listed here are explicitly out-of-scope for the 6-week initial launch and are parked for future phases:

1. **Arabic / RTL Localization**
   - Public site and forms are architected with `locale: 'en'` default hooks in database schemas and content models, ready for future Arabic translation and RTL styles.
2. **AI Chatbot Upgrade**
   - The launch bot is 100% deterministic (decision tree + keyword routing + trigram Q&A bank).
   - In Phase 2, fallback logs from `/admin/chatbot` will inform retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) over client-approved documents.
3. **WhatsApp Automation**
   - Extend the decision tree engine to WhatsApp Business API.
4. **External CRM Integration**
   - Direct webhook or API sync with Salesforce, HubSpot, or Zoho CRM once lead volume justifies it.
5. **Blog & CMS System**
   - Editorial article management and dynamic news publishing.
6. **New Case Study Production & Full Rebrand**
   - Creation of brand-new marketing case studies and collateral.
